# Furbot look mechanics

Furbot keeps its red wheeled lower chassis anchored at a stable baseline. Its white cube head is the primary aiming structure: the eyes lead, the head pitches for up/down and yaws for screen-left/screen-right, while the short spring antennae follow with restrained secondary motion. The gray segmented arms remain attached and shift only enough to preserve balance. The front red panel and white F marking remain identity-locked and must not flip or migrate.

- 000 up: front remains readable; pupils and eyelids aim upward; cube head tips slightly back; more underside of the brow/face plane is visible; antennae trail backward.
- 090 screen-right: head and face yaw toward the viewer's right; nose/face center and pupils cross right of head center; left side plane becomes more visible; right side is slightly occluded.
- 180 down: pupils and eyelids aim downward; cube head tips slightly forward; top plane becomes more visible; antennae follow forward.
- 270 screen-left: head and face yaw toward the viewer's left; nose/face center and pupils cross left of head center; right side plane becomes more visible; left side is slightly occluded.

Each 22.5-degree step uses an even portion of the adjacent cardinal motion. No whole-sprite rotation, scale changes, chassis sliding, replacement eyes, detached effects, shadows, labels, or props.
