# Furbot look mechanics

The deep-navy rectangular torso and ochre wheel/base stay anchored at one baseline. The golden cube head is a rigid aiming structure: the black pupils move within the original white rectangular eye panels, then the whole cube head pitches or yaws slightly. The two blue F-shaped antennae remain attached and follow the head as rigid pieces. Olive angular arms and cobalt spherical hands remain attached and shift only subtly for balance. The dark-blue F-shaped torso marking never mirrors or migrates.

- 000 up: pupils at the image-top of their white panels; head pitches back, revealing slightly more lower face plane and less top plane.
- 090 screen-right: pupils clearly on the image-right side of the eye panels; face yaws to image-right, exposing more of the image-left head side plane.
- 180 down: pupils at the image-bottom of their white panels; head pitches forward, revealing more golden top plane.
- 270 screen-left: pupils clearly on the image-left side of the eye panels; face yaws to image-left, exposing more of the image-right head side plane.

Every 22.5-degree step interpolates evenly. No whole-sprite rotation, chassis slide, scale pop, replacement eyes, flipped torso F, detached effects, shadows, labels, or props.
